# Why choose Continental: content rationale

The user requested a prominent second-screen trust section, viewed through the practical concerns of New Zealand developers. “80%” was treated as a request for broadly relevant concerns, not as a statistical claim.

The six points are an editorial synthesis, not a survey or independently verified company track record:

1. Cost visibility: written scope, allowances, exclusions and stage payments support a meaningful budget.
2. Programme visibility: milestones, trade sequencing, materials and decisions affect planning.
3. Variations: understand cost and time implications and agree changes in writing.
4. Verifiable quality: specifications, applicable inspections and records, with durability/maintenance in view.
5. Accountability: confirm the site contact, update cadence and issue-resolution process.
6. Handover: walkthrough, outstanding items and applicable completion/maintenance/warranty documents.

The copy is intentionally framed as matters to agree for the specific project. It does not promise a fixed price, an unconditional completion date, consent approval, finance outcomes, particular licences, membership, insurance coverage or an invented warranty.

Before using stronger evidence-based claims, add the company's verified licence/insurance details, permissioned project evidence and real customer references. The current call to action invites this due diligence.
