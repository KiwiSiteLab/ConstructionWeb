WHY CHOOSE CONTINENTAL

Your investment.
Our focus.

A building project is a big commitment. You deserve clarity on the cost, confidence in the programme and a clear line to the people doing the work.

For Auckland homeowners and developers,
these are the conversations we put first.

Let’s talk through your plans ↗

Clarity on cost.

Define the scope, allowances, exclusions and stage payments in writing. A clear starting point helps you assess the investment and plan your cash flow.

Scope & itemised pricing

A programme you can plan around.

Talk through construction milestones, trade sequencing and long-lead materials early, with visibility of decisions or delays that could affect your completion date.

Milestones & progress updates

Changes with a clear paper trail.

Understand the cost and timing implications of a proposed change, then agree the variation in writing before the changed work goes ahead.

Written variation approvals

Quality you can check.

Agree the material specifications, inspection stages and site records for your project. Good decisions consider durability and maintenance alongside the initial price.

Specifications & inspection records

People you can speak to.

Establish who is responsible on site, how often you’ll hear from them and how issues will be resolved. Clear ownership keeps decisions moving.

Named contact & agreed updates

A considered handover.

Plan for a final walkthrough, an agreed outstanding-items list and the applicable warranties, maintenance information and completion records.

Handover checklist & follow-up
PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIALS

Qualified people.
Confidence you can check.

Our team includes Licensed Building Practitioners and BCITO-qualified tradespeople. We also support the next generation through BCITO apprentice training.

LBP licensing recognises an individual practitioner’s assessed competence. BCITO qualifications and training reflect investment in trade skills. Ask us for the practitioner and licence details relevant to your project.

Check the official LBP register ↗
Confidence starts before the build.

Ask us about relevant work, the team for your project, and applicable licensing and insurance details. Let’s put the right information in front of you before you commit.
