Website privacy.

This page describes the features included in this version of the Continental Construction Ltd website.

Browsing the website

This site does not include analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, user accounts, or cookies. Images and fonts are served with the site, rather than loaded from third-party services.

Calling or texting us

The phone and text links open your device’s calling or messaging app. The website itself does not submit or store an enquiry. Your device and service provider handle calls and messages.

Hosting

The service hosting this website may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the site. Its own policies apply to that processing.
